// sanitized decision records

ADR: Digest-pinned releases

  • Context: field systems need reproducible deployments and traceability.
  • Decision: release immutable OCI image digests with source and revision metadata.
  • Result: every deployment can be mapped back to reviewed source and qualified artifacts.

ADR: Offline-first edge operation

  • Context: field connectivity can be intermittent or unavailable.
  • Decision: keep critical operation local and treat cloud connectivity as an enhancement.
  • Result: local buffering, controlled activation, and last-known-good recovery reduce field risk.

ADR: Standardized field hubs

  • Context: repeated device setup creates drift and slows deployments.
  • Decision: provision a standard hub image and automate OS, packages, containers, permissions, and services.
  • Result: repeatable field environments with a smaller operational support surface.

ADR: MQTT at the edge boundary

  • Context: devices and services need a lightweight, decoupled communication boundary.
  • Decision: use MQTT for telemetry, configuration, inventory, and device-state messaging.
  • Result: producers and consumers can evolve independently while retaining observable message flows.
